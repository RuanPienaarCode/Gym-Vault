Straight-bar Dips

Strengthchesttricepsbar
  1. Support yourself on top of a single straight bar, hands just outside your hips, arms locked.
  2. Lean slightly forward and bend the elbows until the bar reaches your lower chest / upper abs.
  3. Press back to a full lockout at the top.

Cue: the other half of the muscle-up — the top-out is exactly this position.

Trade-offs

Wins

Genuinely better for evening training — no white flash between sets, and lime is the only bright thing on screen so the active set, the running clock and the next rep target find your eye instantly. It also sits naturally inside a dark Obsidian theme, which is where the plugin probably lives most of the time.

Costs

It breaks the white + lime identity Ruan already picked, so it can only ship as the dark half of a pair — which means every surface has to be designed twice and kept in sync. Glow effects also cost contrast discipline: the moment lime is used for small body text on near-black it looks great but pulls attention away from the numbers that matter.

Mapping onto src/styles.css

  • This is largely the existing .theme-dark .gv-app block pushed further: --gv-bg #14180f → #0b0e08, --gv-surface #1b2114 → #141a0f, and a new --gv-line-2 for the raised borders. No new markup.
  • Add --gv-glow: 0 0 18px rgba(163,230,53,.32) and apply it only to three things — the start button, the running clock, and .gv-log-set.done .gv-set-done. Everything else stays flat, which is what keeps the glow meaningful.
  • .gv-hero's gradient is replaced by a radial lime wash at 20% alpha over --gv-surface; drop the 0 10px 30px rgba(132,204,22,.35) shadow, which reads as smear rather than glow on near-black.
