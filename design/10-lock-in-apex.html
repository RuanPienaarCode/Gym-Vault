10 · Lock-In Apex

Fusion of 07 Lock In + 09 Apex. 07's poster layouts and forward-leaning slabs on 09's graphite, lime and gamified systems: level, XP, PB-in-reach, rings, medals.

All directions

Intent

Keep 07's layouts (one giant word or number per screen, slanted slabs, a poster hero, a tilted share card) and make them serious and green: graphite surfaces, one lime accent, and progress you can read at a glance.

Gamification lives inside the poster layouts as slim slabs and bars, not as bento tiles, so Today stays about one screen long.

Palette

Lime is THE accent (action, progress, PB, level); black text on lime is 14:1. Amber is for streaks and gold medals only. Glow is reserved for PB and level-up moments. Orange is gone.

Trade-offs

Wins

It keeps 07's composition (one giant thing per screen, a clear place to tap) and 09's promise that every screen answers "how close am I" with a bar, a percentage and an "X to go". Dark plus lime reads as serious; the forward lean and the ticker keep it from reading as a spreadsheet.

Costs

  • Lime now does four jobs (action, progress, PB, level), and 07's one big colour flood is gone. The only full lime areas are the ticker, the share card and the buttons, so a screen can feel less dramatic than 07. Glow and amber are rationed to keep lime meaningful; if amber spreads past streaks and gold, the system blurs.
  • Today is compact only by discipline. Level, PB-in-reach, streak and quest each get one slim row. Each extra row spends the poster hero, so new layers need to replace something rather than stack. XP rules, the level curve, quest generation and medal tiers are new product scope and need defining before these numbers are real.
  • Nag risk and font weight. "Don't break it" and "PB in reach" need a celebrate / plain switch and should only appear when the week is at risk. Barlow Condensed must be bundled for offline Obsidian mobile, its fallback only fakes the italic, and parallelogram clipping hides borders and focus rings, so production needs wrapper elements for those.

Mapping onto src/styles.css

  • Reuse 09's token set (--lime, --amber, --steel, tile gradient) and 07's --slant clip-path. The hex emblem replaces 07's sticker for streak, level and PB; one class covers all three.
