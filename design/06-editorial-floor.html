Straight-bar Dips

  1. Support yourself on top of a single straight bar, hands just outside your hips, arms locked.
  2. Lean slightly forward and bend the elbows until the bar reaches your lower chest / upper abs.
  3. Press back to a full lockout at the top.

Cue: the other half of the muscle-up — the top-out is exactly this position.

Trade-offs

Wins

Energy is now a scarce resource instead of a background hum: there is exactly one lime flood per screen and it always sits under your thumb, so "what do I do now" is answered by colour before you read a word. The editorial masthead still tells you what today is in one glance, and the logging screen keeps 48px targets and hard-edged rows for sweaty mid-set taps — the two things each parent was actually best at.

Costs

It's a two-register design, so it needs discipline to extend: every new screen has to decide whether it's a reading surface or an acting surface, and a careless third register (Plans, History, Profile) will make it look inconsistent rather than intentional. The 62px display cap is a compromise — it's smaller than 01's cover-line drama and bigger than 03's slab type, so neither parent's peak moment is fully preserved. The condensed webfont is still the weak link on iOS.

Mapping onto src/styles.css

  • Split the hero. .gv-hero loses its gradient and becomes a plain white masthead; the lime gradient moves to a new .gv-hero-action wrapper that holds only .gv-btn-hero. That's one new element in page-dashboard.js and keeps --gv-lime doing exactly one job per screen.
  • One new token, four call sites. Add --gv-display (Anton, with the Impact/Arial-Narrow fallback chain) and route only .gv-hero-title, .gv-tile-big, .gv-ex-name and .gv-goal-nums b at it. Ship the woff2 inside the plugin folder and reference it with a relative @font-face — iOS has none of the fallbacks, and Obsidian mobile is frequently offline.
  • Two border registers, not one. Reading surfaces keep 1px solid var(--gv-line) with --gv-radius: 0 and --gv-shadow: none; acting surfaces (.gv-log-set, .gv-set-input, .gv-set-done, .gv-bar) go to 2px solid var(--gv-ink). .gv-bar grows 8px → 14px with a repeating-gradient stripe on .gv-bar-fill — same width maths from goalProgress(). Every new button colour rule still needs the .gv-app ancestor to beat app.css in light mode.
