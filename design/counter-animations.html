Counters that hit back

Eighteen animation treatments for counters and progress bars, in the Editorial Floor palette and type registers so they drop into the plugin without a restyle. Every one is CSS transforms and opacity only, guarded for reduced motion, and safe on the iOS 15 WebKit floor. Pick per surface — the loud ones lose their meaning if everything is loud.

Picking one

The catalogue is deliberately wider than what should ship. Two loud treatments on one screen cancel each other out — the same failure mode the design's "exactly ONE lime flood per screen" rule already guards against. Suggested split:

On the loud ones

11 and 12 stack every technique in this file — indeterminate load, energy cells, flames, then a full-bar ascension. That is the point of them and also the warning: they cost roughly 30 concurrently animating nodes at max, they shake the layout by 1.5px, and they read as a reward. A reward that fires twice in a session is a decoration, and a decoration that shakes is a bug. Gate them on something that genuinely happens rarely, and pick one — fire and electric are the same idea at two temperatures, so running both just tells the user the app has two ways of being excited.

Porting into the plugin

  • Build DOM with dom.js's el() / createEl, never innerHTML — this file uses it only because it is a standalone page, and the plugin's review gate rejects it.
  • Every rule that colours a <button> needs the .gv-app ancestor to beat app.css's button:not(.clickable-icon) at (0,1,1). The punch counter and the pips are both buttons in practice.
  • Dark-theme variants go on .theme-dark .gv-app, matching the existing token block.

iOS 15 WebKit floor

  • Everything here is transforms, opacity, gradients, clip-path-free overflow and SVG stroke-dashoffset — all supported on the real floor, not just minAppVersion.
  • Deliberately avoided: @property (16.4), color-mix() (16.2), :has() (15.4), oklch, and regex lookbehind anywhere near the bundle.
  • mask-image on the power-up rays carries its -webkit- prefix; without it Safari 15 paints the full disc.

Motion budget

  • Only transform and opacity are animated, so nothing here triggers layout. Licks, embers and sparks are the only places with many concurrently animating nodes — capped at 7/6 on the flame bar and about 30 on 11 at max, and all are display: none under reduced motion.
  • Staggered fills (energy, pips, chevrons, ascension) run off one elapsed-time rAF driver rather than a chain of setTimeouts. A chain drifts under load and stretches to one step per second in a hidden tab, where timers clamp to 1s; the driver lands the sequence in the duration it claims and catches up in a single frame after a stall.
  • prefers-reduced-motion collapses durations to ~0 rather than removing the effect, so the state change still lands. A counter that silently stops updating is worse than one that snaps.
  • Infinite animations (flame, pulse, heartbeat) must be torn down when the view closes, or they keep the compositor awake in a background leaf.
