Straight-bar Dips

Strengthchesttricepsbar
  1. Support yourself on top of a single straight bar, hands just outside your hips, arms locked.
  2. Lean slightly forward and bend the elbows until the bar reaches your lower chest / upper abs.
  3. Press back to a full lockout at the top.

Cue: the other half of the muscle-up — the top-out is exactly this position.

Trade-offs

The lowest-risk evolution — it looks like the plugin you already have, with the noise removed.

Wins

Reads calmly at 390px and at desktop width without re-thinking the layout, ages well, and never fights an Obsidian theme. Everything is already in the token set, so it's the cheapest direction to ship and the easiest to extend to Plans, History and Profile consistently.

Costs

Least memorable of the five — nothing here says "athlete" on its own; the personality has to come from the copy and the content. Hairline-only separation gets fragile on low-contrast screens and in bright gym lighting, where the current card borders do more work.

Mapping onto src/styles.css

  • Set --gv-shadow: none and lighten --gv-line to #eaeee1; .gv-card / .gv-tile keep their class names but lose box-shadow, so the diff is a handful of declarations rather than new markup.
  • .gv-hero drops the lime gradient for a border-left: 3px solid var(--gv-lime) rail on white; .gv-btn-hero becomes background: var(--gv-lime-deep); color:#fff — that pairing is 5.0:1, so the CTA finally passes AA where lime-on-white text never could.
  • .gv-tiles switches from bordered cards to a hairline grid (border-top + border-left on .gv-tile), and --gv-radius eases 16px → 14px. Every dark-theme override in the file keeps working untouched.
