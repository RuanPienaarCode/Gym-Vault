07 · Lock In

Sport-brand app energy pushed into 2026: black-and-white base, giant condensed italics, one hot orange, stickers and short cheeky copy. Rest is quiet; action is loud.

All directions

Intent

Make logging feel like a drop, not a spreadsheet. Type is the photography: one giant word per screen (PUSH, 8, PB), a full-bleed orange block wherever you act, and a sticker whenever you earn something.

Built entirely from type, colour, slants and gradients, so it works with zero user photos. Nothing here uses a logo, a swoosh or a brand slogan.

Palette

Monochrome does the work; orange is the only hue. Black text sits on orange (6:1); white text never does.

Trade-offs

Wins

Every screen has one word or number you can read from across the gym (PUSH, 8, PB, 4:58), and one place the colour goes. Rewards are the loudest thing in the app, which is the right place for them, and the share card gives the plugin a reason to be seen outside Obsidian.

Costs

  • Loud is tiring on reading surfaces. It suits Today, the live set and the recap. Exercise how-to text, the plan editor and Settings would need a calm register (this file's Records and Running screens are the ceiling for density), or the app will shout at people who are just reading.
  • The font is load-bearing. The look depends on a true condensed italic. Obsidian mobile is often offline, so Barlow Condensed (OFL) has to ship inside the plugin folder; the Impact / Arial Narrow fallback is only condensed, and its italic is faked by the browser. Orange is also unusable for small text on white (3.3:1), so on light themes it is limited to large type and graphics, as on screen E.
  • Voice and motion are opinionated. Short, cheeky copy ("Told you.", "Lock in") and wobbling stickers will delight some people and grate on others; they need a plain-copy setting and a reduced-motion path (this file already honours prefers-reduced-motion). Parallelogram clipping also cuts off focus rings and shadows, so production needs a wrapper element for those.

Mapping onto src/styles.css

  • One accent token, --gv-lime to the orange, plus a --gv-slant clip-path used by .gv-btn, chips and the week cells. The sticker is a single clip-path class that the streak, the PB and the Running record all reuse.
