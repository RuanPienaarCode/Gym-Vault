Five directions for a training tracker that means it — plus the one we're building

Same three views in every file — Today, Exercises & detail, and a live logging screen — so the directions can be compared on identical content rather than on how good their filler looks. Real data throughout: the Get Over The Bar program, Day B, week streak 3, plank at 2:45 of 6:00.

Decision: Ruan picked 01 Editorial and 03 Training Floor. 06 Editorial Floor is the fusion and is now the direction to build; 01 and 03 stay here as the source material.

Round two (10 Oct 2026): three louder 2026 / Gen Z directions — 07 Nike-inspired, 08 Adidas-inspired, 09 brand-free premium + gamified (Apex). Focus groups liked 07 and 09, so 10 Lock-In Apex fuses them: 07’s layout in 09’s graphite + lime with its game systems. Each shows Today, the live set, a shareable session-complete card and Records, on fictional sample data.

Chosen: 06 Editorial Floor Brand: white + lime, one dark counterpoint Accent: #84cc16 · text lime #4d7c0f Target: 390px iPhone pane → desktop Grade: mockup, not production

The chosen direction

Chosen · building this

06

Editorial Floor

Fusion of 01 Editorial + 03 Training Floor

Editorial calm is the page's resting state; floor energy is rationed and spent only where you act — one lime flood per screen, always under your thumb. The masthead answers "what is today" in a capped condensed headline, stats stay on a quiet hairline grid, and the logging screen goes fully hard-edged with 48px targets.

Wins
Colour becomes a wayfinding signal instead of decoration — you can see where to act before you read anything. Keeps 01's one-glance hierarchy and 03's mid-set ergonomics without inheriting either one's fatigue.
Costs
Two registers need discipline to extend — Plans, History and Profile each have to be classified as a reading or an acting surface, and the 62px display cap is a compromise that gives up 01's full cover-line drama.

10

Lock-In Apex

Fusion of 07 Lock In + 09 Apex

07’s skeleton in 09’s skin. The layouts, the one giant word or number per screen, the forward-leaning parallelogram slabs, Barlow Condensed italic, the ticker strip and the tilted share card all come from 07. The graphite base, lime as the single accent, amber for streaks and gold medals, and the game systems come from 09: level and XP bar, “PB in reach” on Today, “BEAT 12 · you’re on 6” on the live set, the struck old PB → huge new PB, “+240 XP” filling the bar, a weekly quest, and hex medals with progress on the locked ones.

Wins
Keeps 07’s poster energy and tight one-screen Today while making progress and PBs unmissable — the two things each parent was missing. Hex emblems replace the starburst, so it stays premium rather than cartoonish.
Costs
Lime does four jobs (act, progress, PB, level), so amber and glow must stay rationed or it blurs; less colour drama than 07’s orange floods. XP rules, level curve, quests and medal tiers are new product scope. Barlow Condensed must ship as woff2; clip-path slabs need wrappers for focus rings.

Round two · 2026 Gen Z explorations · 10 Oct 2026

07

Lock In

Inspired by Nike · codename Just Do It

Monochrome base, one electric-orange accent, and every slab, chip and button cut as a parallelogram so the whole UI leans forward. Each screen spends itself on one giant word or number (PUSH, 8, PB, 4:58); starburst stickers mark streaks and PBs; copy is short and cheeky.

Wins
Maximum hype on the three screens that need it — Today, the live set and the shareable PB card — and the forward slant reads as motion with no imagery at all.
Costs
Wears on reading surfaces (how-to text, plan editor). Needs Barlow Condensed bundled for offline iPhone; orange on white is only 3.3:1; clip-path clips focus rings unless wrapped.

08

Three Lines

Inspired by Adidas · performance black + solar

Modern activewear: black and charcoal with white type, solar red for every action (start, current set, today, active tab, PB stamp) and signal blue for every figure (meters, heatmap, pace). Three equal white stripes are the graphic system; squad-number tiles mark the lineup, a race bib holds the best 5K, and Session complete is a white matchday ticket with a red "NEW PB" stamp. Upright and square — no slant.

Wins
Strict colour roles — red means act, blue means data — so the screen reads before you do. The ticket is the most ownable share moment of the set, and it maps onto --gv-* as a palette preset plus a display-font swap.
Costs
At first glance close to 07 (black + hot accent); it stays apart only through red+blue, upright geometry and the stripe/ticket system. No colour left for destructive actions; white sheets glare at night on OLED; Archivo must ship as woff2.

09

Apex

Brand-free · 2026 premium, gamified · reworked from "Main Character" → "Graphite"

Graphite with heavy, skewed Geist 900 headlines and one huge number per tile. Every screen answers "how close am I" with a thick lime ring or bar, a percentage and an "X to go" line. Levels and XP (+240 XP fills the bar on Session complete), a prominent amber streak, weekly quests, hex medals in bronze/silver/gold with progress on the locked ones, and a PB moment that gets the biggest type in the app: 12 → 14.

Wins
Progress and PBs are unmissable on every screen, and the reward loop (XP, streak, quests, medals) feels premium rather than toy-like. Lime means act/progress, amber means streak/medal — two colours, clear jobs.
Costs
XP rules, level curve, quests and badge tiers are new product scope, not a reskin. Today runs ~two screens; streak nudges can nag (needs a plain setting). Glow + skew cost on iOS 15 is unproven; Geist must ship as woff2.

How to read these

Every file stacks the same three views so you're comparing structure, not content. Scroll each one on a narrow window first — that's the real target — then widen it to check the desktop pane.

What the hybrid resolved

  • Kept from 01 — the capped condensed masthead answering "what is today", oversized numerals on a hairline grid, the lime highlighter sweep, and the whitespace discipline that makes the rest of the page quiet.
  • Kept from 03 — the lime flood with its diagonal cut (now a slab that holds only the CTA), the striped progress bars, the hard-edged 2px set rows with 48px tick targets, and the motivational voice at two points per screen.
  • Dropped — 01's 84px cover type (capped at 62px so a 390px pane doesn't scroll excessively) and 01's shadowless-everything austerity on the logging screen; 03's global 3px black borders, its skewed block chips, and most of its microcopy.

Still open for later

  • 06 + 05 — Editorial Floor with Stat Sheet's mono tabular numerals for every figure. One new token (--gv-mono), no markup change.
  • 06 + 04 — Night Session becomes 06's .theme-dark block rather than a separate design, so the plugin follows the Obsidian theme.

What these are not

Mockup grade: no state handling, no empty/error/loading cases, no responsive polish past two breakpoints, and Tailwind-free hand-written CSS that is not meant to be lifted into src/styles.css as-is. Each file's footer lists how its ideas map onto the existing tokens; that mapping is the handoff, not the CSS.
