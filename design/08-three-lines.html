08 · Three Lines

Proposed

Activewear kit on a technical grid: black and white blocks, solar red for action, signal blue for data, and three parallel lines as the graphic system.

All directions

Intent

A training kit, not a dashboard. Every stat is a squad number, every session ends as a ticket you would actually post, and the only ornament is three equal parallel lines: dividers, 3-bar meters, jersey sleeves, the active tab. Colour has two jobs only: red means act, blue means measure.

Photography-free by design: energy comes from type, colour and stripes. The lines are white on black, always equal length and never ascending, so they stay a generic pattern. Geometry is square and upright (no slant, no italics), and white paper cards (ticket, bib, kit sheet) sit on black, which keeps it apart from 07's single-accent, forward-leaning look.

Palette & type

  • Black #0E0E10
  • Charcoal #1C1C20
  • Line #3A3A42
  • White #FFFFFF
  • Solar red #FF3B1F
  • Signal blue #2E7BFF

Archivo, one variable family used at two widths: Expanded 900 for words, headings and buttons; Condensed 900 for jersey numerals. Body is Archivo 500. Fallbacks are Arial Black (wide) and Impact (tall), both present on iOS.

Text on red is black (5.4:1), never white (3.6:1). Secondary greys are #A1A1AA on dark and #55555E on white. Blue is used for large type and graphics; small blue text on dark uses #8DB6FF. The heatmap ramps charcoal, #1B3F8A, #2460C9, #2E7BFF, with #8DB6FF for the peak day.

Trade-offs

  • Close to 07 on the surface. Black plus a hot accent is the same first impression. 08 stays apart only through the red-plus-blue pairing, the upright square geometry and the white-stripe and paper-card system; if those get diluted it collapses into 07.
  • Strict roles leave no spare colour. Red is action and achievement, blue is data, so there is nothing left for warnings or destructive actions. Red for "End session" or "Delete" would fight the red PB stamp and CTA.
  • White sheets are bright. The kit sheets and ticket give hard colour-blocking, but a large white area at night on an OLED phone glares, and a fixed kit ignores the Obsidian theme. Archivo needs a bundled woff2 for offline iPhone use.

Mapping onto src/styles.css

  • It is a palette preset plus one display token. The kit maps onto the existing token set: --gv-bg / --gv-surface become black and charcoal, --gv-lime becomes solar red (as the yellow preset at line 2633 already swaps it), a new data token carries signal blue, and --gv-display points at Archivo. No new layout engine is needed.
  • Two new primitives. .gv-tl (the three-line divider, a single gradient) and a three-row variant of the existing .gv-bar. Both are pure CSS and reuse the width maths from goalProgress().
  • The ticket is a new component, not a restyle. The session-complete card and the PB stamp would be new markup in page-session.js; the share-as-image step needs a canvas render, which this mockup does not prove.
